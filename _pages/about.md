I am a mechanical engineer and M.Sc. student in Energy Conversion at Sharif University of Technology. My work combines numerical and semi-analytical modeling, CFD simulation (Ansys Fluent, STAR-CCM+), optimization, and experimental setup development for thermal and energy systems.

My research focuses on **energy storage and sustainable thermal systems** — thermoelectric devices and heat exchangers, dropwise condensation, heat pumps, and atmospheric water harvesting. I am seeking a **PhD position starting fall 2026**.

I have authored journal and conference papers on thermoelectric heat exchangers, atmospheric water harvesting, dropwise condensation, and heat pump systems, and I have served as a teaching assistant and lab instructor for eleven course offerings across thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and thermal systems design at Sharif University of Technology and Isfahan University of Technology.
