Hossein Moosavi Shoa'r

M.Sc. candidate in Energy Conversion at Sharif University of Technology — thermal systems, heat transfer, and energy engineering.

Isfahan, Iran

I am a mechanical engineer and M.Sc. student in Energy Conversion at Sharif University of Technology. My work combines numerical and semi-analytical modeling, CFD simulation (Ansys Fluent, STAR-CCM+), optimization, and experimental setup development for thermal and energy systems.

My research focuses on energy storage and sustainable thermal systems — thermoelectric devices and heat exchangers, dropwise condensation, heat pumps, and atmospheric water harvesting. I am seeking a PhD position starting fall 2026.

About Me

A passionate thermal engineer based in Isfahan, Iran

Beyond the equations, my work is about building things that measure, move, and convert energy. I have authored journal and conference papers on thermoelectric heat exchangers, atmospheric water harvesting, dropwise condensation, and heat pump systems — and I love the moment when a model finally matches the experiment.

Teaching is the other half of my academic life: I have served as a teaching assistant and lab instructor for eleven course offerings across thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and thermal systems design. Off campus you will find me hiking, traveling, or tinkering with Arduino.
